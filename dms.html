How it works

Each line takes an angle in any of these forms: 45°30'15", 45 30 15, 45-30-15, 45:30:15 or 45d30m15s. Decimal minutes and seconds work too, like 45°30.25' or 12 04 30.5.

Plain numbers are read the way the dropdown says. Pick DD.MMSS to match HP and Casio survey calculators, where 45.3015 means 45°30'15".

A negative sign on a line works the same as flipping it to −. The result keeps its sign and can go over 360°. Use the Wrapped row when you're carrying an azimuth around a traverse.

Rounding happens once, on the final answer, so adding many lines doesn't build up error. For bearings, use the Angle Converter to turn them into azimuths first.
