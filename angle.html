What it understands

DMS with symbols or separators: 45°30'15", 45 30 15, 45-30-15, 45:30:15, 45d30m15s. Decimal minutes and seconds work too, like 45°30.25'.

Bearings: N 45-30-15 E, S12°04'30"W. The angle has to be 90° or less. The DMS and decimal results are for the bearing's angle, so S 12-04-30 W gives 12.075°.

Plain numbers are read the way the dropdown says. Pick DD.MMSS to match HP and Casio survey calculators, where 45.3015 means 45°30'15". Extra digits are decimal seconds: 45.301525 is 45°30'15.25".

A plain angle without N/S and E/W is treated as a direction clockwise from north, so 135 gives a bearing of S 45°00'00" E. Negative angles and angles over 360° are wrapped around for the bearing. The DMS and decimal results keep the value you typed.
