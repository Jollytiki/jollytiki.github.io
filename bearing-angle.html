How it works

Interior is the smaller angle between the two lines, 180° or less. Exterior is the rest of the circle, 360° minus the interior. Together they always total 360°.

Both leave the corner is for two lines that leave the same point, like a NW line and a SW line drawn from one corner. The angle is measured between them exactly as written.

Line 1 arrives, line 2 leaves is for a traverse or a deed, where you arrive on line 1 and leave on line 2. Line 1 is turned around (its back bearing) before the angle is measured. The deflection angle is how far you turn, right or left, from the direction you were heading.

Angle right is turned clockwise from line 1 to line 2, the way most total stations turn angles. Angle left is turned counterclockwise. Bearings can be written N 45-30-15 W, N45°30'15"W or N45.3015W. A plain number is an azimuth from north.
