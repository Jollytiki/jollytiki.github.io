How it works

Each course is walked out from the start point. The error of closure is the gap between where the last course lands and where it should land: the start point for a loop, or the known point you entered. Precision is the total length divided by that gap, written 1:x.

The Compass (Bowditch) rule spreads the error along the traverse in proportion to distance. A point halfway around gets half the correction. The adjusted bearings and distances are inversed from the adjusted coordinates.

For a loop, the area comes from the adjusted coordinates. This tool works from bearings. If you have turned angles, the Angle Between Bearings tool converts between the two. For checking a deed description, try MapCheck. For the whole calculation worked by hand, see the field note Traverse adjustment, step by step.
