MapCheck

Type in a deed's courses and MapCheck plots the figure and tells you whether it closes.

  • Error of closure, precision ratio and enclosed area
  • Quadrant bearings, distances and circular curves
  • Opens .MAP files from the original 1992 QuickBASIC program
  • Runs on your own computer. No account, no upload, nothing leaves your machine
