How it works

Give three of the six values, with at least one side. Three angles alone fix the shape of a triangle but not its size. Angles take any common format: 45°30'15", 45 30 15, 45-30-15 or decimal degrees.

SSS (three sides) and SAS (two sides and the angle between them) use the law of cosines. ASA and AAS (two angles and a side) find the third angle from the 180° total, then use the law of sines.

SSA (two sides and an angle that isn't between them) is the ambiguous case. Depending on the numbers there can be no triangle, one, or two. When two triangles fit, both are shown, and you have to pick the one that matches the field.

Area comes from ½ · b · c · sin A. Heights are measured from each vertex square to the opposite side.
